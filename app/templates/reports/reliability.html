Definitions.
  • Failure: a repair (corrective) work order opened in the period. Cancelled jobs are ignored.
  • MTTR (mean time to repair): average downtime of completed repair jobs.
  • MTBF (mean time between failures): total operating time divided by the number of failures, where operating time is the hours each active device existed in the period minus its repair downtime.
  • Availability: operating time as a share of total time.
  • Preventive maintenance downtime is not counted. Devices without a purchase date are assumed to exist for the whole period. Types with no failures show a dash for MTBF.
